# Spaza-Stock-Sales-Tracker
A simple web application that helps spaza shops and small retailers manage inventory, track sales, and monitor stock levels in real time. The system automates stock updates, provides low-stock alerts, generates sales reports, and offers smart restocking suggestions to help business owners make informed decisions and reduce manual record-keeping.
Features
User authentication
Inventory management
Real-time stock tracking
Sales recording
Low-stock alerts
Sales reports and analytics
Smart restocking suggestions
Problem Statement
Many small retailers still use notebooks or manual processes to record stock and sales. This often results in inaccurate stock records, stock shortages, and poor visibility into business performance.

Solution
The Spaza Stock & Sales Tracker digitises inventory and sales management, helping shop owners maintain accurate stock levels, monitor sales trends, and make informed purchasing decisions.
